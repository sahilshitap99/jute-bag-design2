About Vertex Global Exim

Vertex Global Exim is a Mumbai-based merchant exporter specializing in high-quality jute bags for brands and businesses worldwide. We supply eco-friendly packaging solutions to clients across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia, helping companies replace single-use plastic with sustainable alternatives.

Founded by like-minded individuals, Vertex Global Exim was established with a simple mission — to create sustainable packaging that protects the planet. What began as a vision to reduce plastic pollution in India has grown into a trusted global export business, delivering custom jute bags that are strong, reusable, and fully biodegradable.

Our Mission: To replace plastic with sustainable jute and empower a greener future through responsible packaging solutions that are eco-friendly, ethical, and made to last.
